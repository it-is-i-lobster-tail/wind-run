Wind Run offline US area data

History search works out the parks, neighborhoods, towns and states a US workout went through on your iPhone, from this data. Nothing about your workouts is sent anywhere to do it.

Wind Run selected named parks of about 1 hectare or more, and named neighborhoods, localities, regions and territories, intersecting United States map units from Natural Earth (public domain). Wind Run simplified each outline to about 20 m and added an index of geographic bounds for offline queries.
