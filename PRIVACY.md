# Wind Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

## The Short Version

Wind Run does not collect, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never sees your runs.

## What Stays on Your Devices

Your runs, routes, heart rate readings, steps, workouts, coach plans, injury reports and settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server Wind Run runs, because there isn't one.

## Location

Wind Run uses your location while you record a run, including with the screen locked, to map and measure it. Each route is saved with its run on your device. It only leaves your device through iCloud Sync or Save workouts to Health, if you turn those on, or in a file you choose to share.

## Motion & Fitness

If you allow Motion & Fitness, Wind Run counts your steps for cadence, notices when you stop for auto-pause, and reads the barometer to measure hills for climb and calories. These readings are kept with your runs on your device.

## Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on. With Save workouts to Health on, Wind Run adds each finished run or ride to Health as a workout with its distance, route and calories. With your permission it reads heart rate, resting heart rate, steps, distance, running power, and cycling cadence and power during a workout, your weight for calories, and your last 4 weeks of workouts when you set up the coach. Health data is used only to show and measure your runs inside Wind Run. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone. Deleting a run can also remove its workout from Health when you ask. You can change what Wind Run may read or save at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

## Heart Rate Devices

Heart rate comes through Apple's workout session from your Apple Watch, AirPods Pro 3, Powerbeats Pro 2 or a Bluetooth strap paired in iOS Settings. Each run notes which kinds of device recorded it, by hardware model and software version, never by the name you gave the device.

## iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on. With it on, Wind Run keeps a copy of your runs, routes, plans and settings, including your weight, heart rate settings and Starting Point pins, in your own private iCloud database, so a new iPhone can pick up where you left off. Only your Apple Account can read that copy, and Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy. Turning iCloud Sync off deletes the iCloud copy.

## Apple Watch

Wind Run on Apple Watch records runs locally and sends finished runs directly to your paired iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync, the iPhone also syncs those runs to your private iCloud database. Settings such as units, call-outs and haptics, today's coach workouts and your saved workouts pass directly between your iPhone and watch.

## Maps

Route maps are drawn with Apple Maps. To show a map, your device downloads map images for that area from Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your route line is drawn on your device and is not sent to Apple Maps. To show the town a workout started in, and to search by place, Wind Run works out the parks, neighborhoods, towns and states a US workout went through on this iPhone, from map data that comes with the app. Points within 250 m of the start and end are skipped, so your home is never one of them. For a workout that started outside the US, Wind Run asks Apple Maps once for the town at a random point 50 to 150 m from the start, never the start itself. The places are kept only on this iPhone, and are left out of iCloud, backups and exports.

## Weather

Weather for Runs, under Settings → Your Data, is off until you turn it on. With it on, after a run Wind Run asks Apple Weather once for that run's temperature, sky, wind and humidity. It sends only the middle of a square about 5 km across that the run started in, and the time of the run, never your route or where the run started. Apple Weather handles the request under Apple's Privacy Policy. The weather is kept with the run, and goes to iCloud with it if iCloud Sync is on.

## Voice, Haptics and Live Activities

Voice call-outs are spoken on your device from voice clips built into the app, or by the system voice. Haptics are played on your device. The Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is updated by the app on your iPhone, with no push server.

## Exports and Trace Logs

Export Runs saves files only when you ask, and leaves out locations, dates, times, heart rate, names, weight and device names. GPS readings near where each run starts and finishes, 250 m unless you change it, are left out too. Under Settings → Your Data → Starting Point you can pick another distance, or pin places on a map to hide instead, such as home. Pins are kept with your settings and never go in an export. A run's weather goes in the export without its place or time. Trace logging, under Developer Tool, is off unless you turn it on. Its files stay on your iPhone and leave out places, clock times, typed words, names, heart rate and weight. Neither ever leaves your iPhone unless you share it yourself.

## Third Parties

Wind Run contains no third-party code, SDKs or services. It only uses Apple's own services, iCloud, Apple Health, Apple Maps and Apple Weather, as described above. The offline map data comes from Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap contributors and is part of the app, so nothing is sent to them. Settings → Legal → Offline Map Data lists its credits.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete any run in the app. Turning iCloud Sync off deletes the copy in iCloud. Deleting Wind Run from a device deletes everything it kept on that device. Runs saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

## Children

Wind Run is intended only for adults 18 and older.

## Public Source Code

Wind Run's source code is public, so anyone can check this policy against the code at https://github.com/it-is-i-lobster-tail/wind-run.

## Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with a new date. Questions can be raised as an issue on the Wind Run GitHub repository.
